Ricocade

Space pinball for iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store. Last updated: October 13, 2026.

Ricocade is a single-table pinball game built on its own ball physics: a heavy steel ball, snappy flippers, outlanes that punish and a kickback you have to earn. It is meant to feel like the arcade machines that made you put in one more coin.

What is in it

  • One deep space table: orbit, I-O-N lanes, FUEL drop targets, a hyperspace hole and the "Asteroid storm" three-ball multiball with jackpots.
  • 12 missions in 3 tiers and 9 ranks to climb.
  • Skill shot, shot chains and an extra ball for long combos.
  • A daily challenge: the same table and rule twist for everyone, once per UTC day.
  • Game Center leaderboards: all-time classic and daily.

FAQ

Is it free? Yes. The whole table, tier 1 missions, today's daily challenge and all leaderboards are free. One optional purchase (USD 3.99) unlocks all missions and ranks, the daily archive, every table skin and all future tables.

Are there ads or a subscription? No ads, no subscription, no tracking.

Which devices? iPhone with iOS 26 or later, portrait.

Does it work offline? Yes. Leaderboards sync through Game Center when you are online.

Who makes it? An independent developer, Ilker Cem Tanriverdi.

Ricocade Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Summary

Ricocade does not collect, store or share any personal data on our servers. We have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your mission progress, daily challenge results, settings (table skin, simple graphics) and pending scores are saved locally on your device.

Apple services

  • Game Center: if you are signed in to Game Center, your score is sent to Apple's Game Center for leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your personal information.
  • In-app purchase: the one-time full game unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details.

Children

Ricocade does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Son güncelleme: 13 Ekim 2026

Apple hizmetleri

Çocuklar
